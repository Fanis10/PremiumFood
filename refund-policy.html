Return/Refund policy

  • We strive to ensure all our customers are 100% satisfied with our products and service which is why we offer no fuss returns. If you have received a damaged product or incorrect item please contact us and we will refund the amount of the product or replace it in your next order (Please note: this does not apply to changes in product labelling where the product is still the same however changes have been made to the name, description by the manufacturer) If an delivered item is wrong or damaged or is not of acceptable quality please contact us and we will offer a substitute or a full refund. A photo should be provided for the wrong and/or damaged item(s). Any proof of purchase should be provided in order to qualify for a substitute or a full refund of the product. We may request the item be returned before a refund or replacement has been issued on certain items.
  • If you request a substitute or a for a full refund, please contact us at support@allday-supplements.com or phone +44 113 841 8480 and we will provide a return address to send the product/s back to. Please note: return postage is paid by the customer if the delivered items are correct. Full refund will be initiated if the delivered items are wrong or damaged. Fill out the online return form and attach it to your email return request and our courier service will contact you to arrange the pick-up of items.
  • You also have the right to cancel and return your order within 10 business days of the delivery date, for any reason and without a justification. If an incorrect item is received, please contact us as soon as possible. Customers need to ensure the products received are correct before opening which can be verified against the order invoice. If an item is incorrect we will happily exchange for the correct product once the incorrect product has been returned to us unopened. Only once the incorrect item has been returned, we can send the replacement out. Customers are also welcome to a refund once products are returned unopened minus the shipping cost. If a product is missing from the order, please contact us immediately upon receiving the parcel and provide a scanned copy of the invoice. We will issue a refund or store credit for the missing product.
  • Strictly no refunds or exchanges on make up once opened.
  • Shipping costs are non-refundable if the delivered items are correct.
